## Do I need a commercial license?

Most people do **not**. Under the AGPL you can, free of charge:

- use AssetForge at a studio or company, including for commercial games;
- sell, ship and own the assets you make with it (the AGPL covers the program, not its output — the AI services you use have their own terms, see [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md));
- modify AssetForge for internal use;
- share modified versions, as long as you share their source code under the AGPL.

You need a commercial license if you want to:

- include AssetForge code (or a modified version) in a **closed-source** product;
- offer a **modified** AssetForge to others as a hosted or network service **without** publishing your changes (AGPL section 13);
- distribute AssetForge or parts of it under terms other than the AGPL, for example in a proprietary engine plugin or tool suite;
- get warranties, indemnification, or support terms that the AGPL does not give.
